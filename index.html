AgentCore Demo Center: bringing summit demos online

ChallengeAt AWS China Summit, visitors explored AgentCore demos with an expert’s guidance. Bringing them online was an opportunity to make that experience available year-round, at each visitor’s own pace.

  • Approach. Developed a self-serve website where visitors could explore the event demos on their own.
  • Journey. Designed a path from exploring a use case to deployment for developers and tech leads.
  • Content. Wrote the site’s content and created 15+ interactive guides using a spec-driven AI workflow.

ImpactThe demo center became an asset in AWS’s generative AI campaign.

Exploring marketing questions through data

Audience testing on Google Ads

For a protein powder brand, I A/B tested gym and yoga audiences. Neither audience won clearly, but certain keywords did. Rebuilding the campaign around those keywords drove core keyword CTR to 12%.

Finding high-intent shoppers

Using 2.5M+ e-commerce events, I explored how browsing depth, category breadth and click speed relate to purchasing. The model reached 0.95 AUC, with steady engagement emerging as a useful buying signal.

Segmentation for media planning

Using PCA and K-means on Ford Ka consumer research, I identified four psychographic segments. The analysis pointed to fashion-oriented buyers as a priority audience for media planning.

Tools: Python, Google Analytics 4 (certified), Google Ads, Kiro
